Teaching angle: each family has different safety questions. For example, CNNs raise robustness and adversarial-patch issues, Transformers raise explainability and attention-reliability issues, generative models raise hallucination and the validity of synthetic evidence, and multi-task networks raise common-cause failure through the shared backbone. Several mappings, mainly for the temporal and generative rows, are from memory, so verify them before use.

Formats and standards (Ch. 8–9)

  • ASAM standards: OpenLABEL (labels), OpenSCENARIO (scenarios), OpenDRIVE (road networks) and OSI (simulation interfaces).
  • MCAP (Foxglove) and ROS bags for logs.
  • Waymo TFRecord and the nuScenes devkit as vendor formats.
  • Annotation platforms such as Scale AI, Encord, CVAT and understand.ai (dSPACE).

Synthesis tools (Ch. 11)

  • NVIDIA Omniverse and DRIVE Sim, with Cosmos for generative augmentation.
  • Applied Intuition (simulation and data tooling).
  • CARLA (open source), rFpro and Parallel Domain.
  • Waabi World and Waymo SimulationCity.
  • Wayve GAIA (generative scenes).
  • Mobileye REM and Mapillary for crowdsourced data and maps.
  • Foretellix Foretify for scenario coverage.
  • Anonymisation vendors such as Brighter AI.

4. Safety frameworks for Part III

  • Mobileye RSS, which was standardised as IEEE 2846.
  • NVIDIA Halos, a full-stack safety approach (verify current scope).
  • Waymo and Aurora safety-case publications.
  • UL 4600, a safety-case standard for autonomous products.
  • Safety First for Automated Driving (SaFAD), a white paper from Aptiv, Audi, BMW, Continental, Daimler, VW, Baidu and others. It is a good bridge to ISO 8800.
  • Mercedes Drive Pilot (Level 3) as a regulated deployment case study.

5. Suggested priorities

If you can only include a few:

  1. Tesla, Waymo and Wayve for learning methods.
  2. UniAD, BEVFormer and Waymo EMMA for architectures.
  3. Waymo Open Dataset, nuScenes and ASAM OpenLABEL/OpenSCENARIO for data.
  4. NVIDIA Cosmos and Omniverse and CARLA for synthesis.
  5. Mobileye RSS as the safety counterpoint.
