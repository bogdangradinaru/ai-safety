At a glance

LeNet-5Small sequential CNN. Alternates convolution and subsampling, then uses fully connected classification layers.
ResNet-18Deep CNN with residual shortcut connections. The example uses basic two-convolution residual blocks.
EfficientNet-B0Uses MBConv blocks and compound scaling to balance depth, width and input resolution.
YOLOv8Object detector with a CNN backbone, multi-scale FPN/PAN neck and anchor-free detection head.

Important: LeNet-5, ResNet-18 and EfficientNet-B0 are shown with their standard image-classification heads. In a vehicle, they may be adapted or used as backbones in a larger perception system. YOLO is a family rather than one fixed architecture; YOLOv8 is used here as a specific, reproducible example. Architectures and output heads vary across versions and implementations.
